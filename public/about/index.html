Who I Am#

I’m a PhD researcher at Monash University’s ACTION Lab, supervised by Delvin Varghese and Patrick Olivier. My research sits at the intersection of privacy engineering, trusted computing, and human-computer interaction.

Before academia, I spent 5+ years as a software engineer in Seoul, South Korea, building blockchain infrastructure, ZK-rollup systems, and decentralized applications.

Research Focus#

I’m building Epsilon — a privacy-preserving research infrastructure platform that enables researchers to work with sensitive data while maintaining cryptographic guarantees of execution integrity.

The core idea: instead of trusting institutions to handle your data correctly, we provide verifiable proof that your specific code ran on your specific dataset inside a secure enclave — and nothing else leaked.

Key areas:

  • Trusted Execution Environments (AWS Nitro Enclaves)
  • Zero-Knowledge Proofs (Groth16, Circom)
  • AI-assisted data governance (CrewAI, LLMs)
  • Visual schema mapping for data minimization
  • Per-execution cryptographic attestation
